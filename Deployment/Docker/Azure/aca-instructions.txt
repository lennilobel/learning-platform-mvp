:View the logs of the container app in real-time using the Azure CLI. The following command retrieves the last 100 lines of logs from the specified container app:

az containerapp logs show ^
  --resource-group demos-rg ^
  --name ca-learningplatform-api ^
  --type console ^
  --tail 100

az containerapp logs show ^
  --resource-group demos-rg ^
  --name ca-learningplatform-outbox ^
  --type console ^
  --tail 100

az containerapp logs show ^
  --resource-group demos-rg ^
  --name ca-learningplatform-progress ^
  --type console ^
  --tail 100 ^
  --follow
